Unova Leaders Tournament

The Unova Leaders Tournament features the Gym Leaders of Black, White, Black 2, and White 2. All Pokémon are Lv. 50.

Each Trainer below is listed with every Pokémon they can use in this tournament. In Single Battles, trainers will use three; in Double and Rotation Battles they will use four; and in Triple Battles they will use all six. Their lead Pokémon or Ace is always used.

IVs are determined by the Trainer. Ability and gender are random, but will never be Hidden Abilities. No Pokémon in this tournament have EVs.

  • Cheren
  • Roxie
  • Burgh
  • Elesa
  • Clay
  • Skyla
  • Drayden
  • Marlon
  • Chili
