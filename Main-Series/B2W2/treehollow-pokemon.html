White Treehollow/Black Tower Pokémon

The following Pokémon sets are used by the Trainers in the Black Tower and White Treehollow. Each set is used in only one of the two games, so the sets are listed by game and then by the areas they can appear in. Species with more than one set are distinguished with a number, e.g. "Nidoqueen-1" and "Nidoqueen-2", assigned in the order the Pokémon appears in the data.

All sets have 0 IVs and EVs in every stat.

For information about which Trainers can use these Pokémon, see the Black Tower & White Treehollow Trainers page.
