Driftveil and Mix Tournaments

The Driftveil Tournament and the Mix Tournament use the same Trainer rosters and Pokémon. Pokémon are Lv. 25 in the Driftveil Tournament and Lv. 50 in the Mix Tournament. Use the Level dropdown in the calculated-stats view to switch between the two.

Each Trainer below is listed with every Pokémon they can use in this tournament. In Single Battles, Trainers will use the first two listed and one of the other four; in Double and Rotation Battles they will use the first four listed; and in Triple Battles they will use all six.

IVs are determined by the Trainer. Ability and gender are random, but will never be Hidden Abilities. No Pokémon in these tournaments have EVs.
