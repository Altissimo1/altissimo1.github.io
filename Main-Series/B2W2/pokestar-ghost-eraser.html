Ghost Eraser

The Ghost Eraser series is a horror series of 4 movies at Pokéstar Studios, directed by John Pokénter. The mechanics of filming, endings, line prompts, and earnings are explained on the Pokéstar Studios page.

Notation used on this page:

  • [species] and [player] in movie titles and lines are replaced by the species of the first Pokémon the player sends out and the player's name. Text such as [Kid/Girl] changes depending on whether the player is a boy or a girl.
  • Opponents named with a letter and number, such as H1/Brycen-Man, are Props. They appear as green squares while filming and are edited into other forms when the movie is played.
  • LP1, LP2, and so on refer to the first, second, and later line prompts in the movie.
  • Stat columns show each Pokémon's actual stat, with its IV and EV underneath.
  • The opponent always uses the move in the slot chosen by the most recent line prompt. Before the first prompt, it picks moves at random.
