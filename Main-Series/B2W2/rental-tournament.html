Rental Tournament

The Rental Tournament features battles using rental Pokémon. Instead of using their own Pokémon, the both player and every opponent battle with rentals.

It is unlocked by winning the Driftveil Tournament once.

Mechanics

Before the tournament, the player chooses one of three tickets in the lobby. The chosen ticket decides how strong both the player's and opponents' rental Pokémon will be. The player is then offered six rental Pokémon, from which they will pick three in Singles, four in Doubles or Rotations, or all six in Triples. The opposing Trainers will use the same number of Pokémon.

All Pokémon are level 50. The player's Pokémon always have 20 IVs, and opponents' IVs are dependent on the Trainer: Gym Leaders and Bianca have 20 IVs, and all other Trainers have 8 IVs.

Resources

  • Battle Subway Pokémon: every rental Pokémon comes from this list. The details about how Pokémon are chosen are shown below.

Rental Pokémon

Rental Pokémon come from the Pokémon used by Trainers on the Battle Subway's Normal Train. They are split into two pools, Weak and Strong:

Pool Battle Subway sets Pokémon
Weak Low Tier Set-1 and Low Tier Set-2 66 species, 132 sets
Strong Mid Tier Set-1 and Mid Tier Set-2 38 species, 76 sets

Tickets

The ticket the player picks decides which pool(s) the player's six rental Pokémon come from:

Ticket Pokémon offered
Right Weak
Middle Strong
Left 3 weak, 3 strong

The same ticket also decides which pools every opponent's team comes from:

Ticket Single Double Triple Rotation
Right 3 weak 4 weak 6 weak 4 weak
Middle 3 strong 4 strong 6 strong 4 strong
Left 3 strong 3 strong + 1 weak 3 strong + 3 weak 3 strong + 1 weak

Team generation

  • Rental Pokémon are drawn at random from the selected pool each time the player enters. The game will never generate two Pokémon of the same species or two Pokémon holding the same held item in the player's draft or the opponent's team. However, the player and the opponent may share a species or a held item.
  • Ability and gender are random, but will never be Hidden Abilities.
