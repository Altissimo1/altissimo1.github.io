Kanto Leaders Tournament

The Kanto Leaders Tournament features the Gym Leaders of Kanto. All Pokémon are Lv. 50.

Each Trainer below is listed with every Pokémon they can use in this tournament. In Single Battles, trainers will use three; in Double and Rotation Battles they will use four; and in Triple Battles they will use all six. Their lead Pokémon or Ace is always used.

IVs are determined by the Trainer. Ability and gender are random, but will never be Hidden Abilities.

  • Brock
  • Misty
  • Lt. Surge
  • Erika
  • Sabrina
  • Blaine
  • Giovanni
  • Janine
