Black Tower & White Treehollow Trainers

The Black Tower and White Treehollow take their Trainers from a shared list. Each Trainer in the list has a Trainer class, a game version, a range of areas they can appear in, and a Pokémon roster. The game data is not structured to indicate Trainer names, and most rosters seem based on the Trainer class anyway, so Trainer names are not included on this page.

The numbers after the Pokémon's names refer to set numbers, explained in more detail on the Black Tower & White Treehollow Pokémon page.

Trainer Mechanics

  • Each area has a fixed layout that determines how many Trainers of each class appear in it. The layouts for each game are shown below.
  • After the area is generated, one of its Trainers is replaced with a Doctor (Black 2) or a Nurse (White 2).
  • Within a game, every Trainer of the same class in the same areas uses the same roster, so the rosters below are listed by class and area rather than by individual Trainer.
