Black Tower

Access to:
Exit: Black City

Funfest Missions:
No sparkles or mission NPCs appear here.

Overview

This is a training challenge located in Black City. It cannot be challenged until after defeating the Elite Four, and then defeating Alder in Floccesy Town.

In this area, the player will progress through ten procedurally generated areas, each of which has a specific number of Trainers. In each area, a randomly selected Trainer is the gatekeeper Trainer; defeating this Trainer unlocks the door to the Boss Trainer's room, and defeating the Boss Trainer will allow the player to move on to the next area. One Trainer on each floor is always a Doctor, who will offer to heal the player's Pokémon once after they are defeated, and this Doctor will never be the gatekeeper Trainer.

Although this is a battle gauntlet, it bears some differences from facilities such as the Battle Subway, outlined below:

  • The player can use the "Shift" battle style.
  • The player will gain experience and win money for completing battles.
  • It is possible to change Pokémon's held items during the course of the challenge.

The player cannot use items from their bag otherwise, whether in or out of battle, meaning speaking to a Doctor is the only means by which Pokémon are healed.

Except for Boss Trainers, the Trainer classes that will be faced and the Pokémon that they possess are taken randomly from predefined rosters. This page contains all the possible Pokémon that can be encountered (outside of Boss Trainers), and this page contains the possible Trainers that can be encountered and the Pokémon they can have.

Items

The following items can be obtained as rewards for clearing each Area.

Areas 1-5

In Areas 4-5, two of the same type of item will be obtained as the reward.

HP Up Protein Iron Calcium Zinc Carbos

Area 5

Upon clearing Area 5 for the first time, if the player is playing Black 2, they will obtain the Tower Key. Using Unova Link, this can be sent to White 2 players, allowing them to access Black City and Black Tower.

Areas 6-9

In Areas 8-9, two of the same type of item will be obtained as the reward.

Health Wing Muscle Wing Resist Wing Genius Wing Clever Wing Swift Wing Rare Candy

Area 10

Pomeg Berry Kelpsy Berry Qualot Berry Hondew Berry Grepa Berry Tamato Berry Rare Candy
