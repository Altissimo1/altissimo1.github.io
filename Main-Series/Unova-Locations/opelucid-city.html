Opelucid City

Access to:
North: Route 10
West: Route 9
East: Route 11

Places of interest:
Poké Mart
Move Tutor: After defeating the Elite Four, Iris (Black) or Drayden (White) will teach Draco Meteor to a Dragon-type Pokémon with high friendship.
Standing near the keytar (Black) or erhu (White) player west of the Pokémon Center will add a synth (Black) or erhu (White) track to the background music.
Opelucid Gym: The eighth Gym. It specializes in Dragon-types.

Access to:
West: Route 9
East: Route 11
Occasionally accessible from the area: Plasma Frigate

Places of interest:
Poké Mart

Standing near the keytar (Black 2) or erhu (White 2) player west of the Pokémon Center will add a synth (Black 2) or erhu (White 2) track to the background music.
Opelucid Gym: The seventh Gym. It specializes in Dragon-types.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 18 spots. Looks different between Black and White versions, but the spot placements are the same.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.
