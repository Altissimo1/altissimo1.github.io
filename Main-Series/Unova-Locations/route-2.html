Route 2

Access to:
North: Striaton City
East: Accumula Town

Utility:
Cut is required to access a Great Ball and a Super Potion.
Strength is required to access a Rare Candy.

Access to:
North: Striaton City
East: Accumula Town

Utility:
Cut is required to access a PP Max, a Carbos, and Lass Henrietta, as well as four spots for Funfest Missions.
Strength is required to access a Calcium.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 20 spots. Four spots require Cut.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.

Memory Link encounters

After viewing the Memory Link flashback "Meeting Friends, Saying Good-Bye", the player may encounter N's Purrloin here. Its appearance rate depends on how many of N's Pokémon the player has already captured, as shown below.

  • 0 captured: 40%
  • 1-5 captured: 20%
  • 6-11 captured: 10%
  • 12+ captured: 5%
