Route 19

Access to:
South: Aspertia City
East: Floccesy Town

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Water Stone, a Nest Ball, and an Expert Belt, as well as five spots for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 15 spots. Five spots require Surf.
Funfest Trainers here are 1 level lower in Easy Mode and 1 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Politoed have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
