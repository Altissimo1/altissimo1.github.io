Humilau City

Access to:
West: Route 22
South: Route 21
East: Marine Tube

Places of interest:
Poké Mart
Humilau Gym: The eighth Gym. It specializes in Water-type Pokémon.

Utility:
Surf can be used to encounter wild Pokémon.

Funfest Missions:
Forest map with 17 spots. 4 of the spots require using Surf to reach.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Staryu and Starmie have a 50% chance of holding Stardust and a 5% chance of holding a Star Piece.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Corsola have a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Luvdisc have a 50% chance of holding a Heart Scale.
  • Wild Shellder and Cloyster have a 50% chance of holding a Pearl and a 5% chance of holding a Big Pearl.
