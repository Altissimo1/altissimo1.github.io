Access to:
West: Marvelous Bridge
East: Black City (Black 2) or White Forest (White 2)

Places of interest:
Poké Transfer Lab

Utility:
Strength is required to access TM26 Earthquake and an Ultra Ball.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 16 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Fearow have a 5% chance of holding a Sharp Beak.
  • Wild Marowak have a 5% chance of holding a Thick Club.
  • Wild Sawk and Throh have a 5% chance of holding a Black Belt and a 1% chance of holding an Expert Belt (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Mankey have a 5% chance of holding a Payapa Berry.
