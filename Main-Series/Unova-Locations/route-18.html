Route 18

Access to:
East: Route 17

Places of interest:
An Ace Trainer in the house will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.

Utility:
Surf is required to access and traverse the route. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Strength is required to access a Max Elixir, a Calcium, a Dragon Scale, and Backpacker Sam.

Access to:
East: Route 17

Places of interest:
An Ace Trainer in the house will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.

Utility:
Surf is required to access and traverse the route. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Strength is required to access a Zinc, a Rare Candy, a Star Piece, and two Trainers.

Pokémon

  • Wild Scraggy have a 5% chance of holding a Shed Shell.
  • Wild Dwebble have a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Sawk and Throh have a 5% chance of holding a Black Belt and a 1% chance of holding an Expert Belt (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Crustle have a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone and a 1% chance of holding a Rare Bone (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Horsea, Seadra, and Kingdra have a 5% chance of holding a Dragon Scale.
  • Wild Finneon and Lumineon have a 5% chance of holding a Rindo Berry.
  • Wild Chinchou have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Qwilfish have a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
