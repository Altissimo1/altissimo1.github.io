Route 8

Access to:
West: Icirrus City
East: Tubeline Bridge
Accessible on the route: Moor of Icirrus

Utility:
Surf is required in the winter (April, August, and December) to access a Full Restore. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
West: Icirrus City
East: Tubeline Bridge
Accessible on the route: Moor of Icirrus

Utility:
Surf is required in the winter (April, August, and December) to access a Max Repel and a Big Pearl. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Forest map with 20 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Palpitoad and Seismitoad have a 50% chance of holding a Persim Berry.
  • Wild Stunfisk have a 5% chance of holding Soft Sand.
  • Wild Croagunk have a 5% chance of holding Black Sludge.

Walking

Swarm

During a swarm, the player has a 40% chance of encountering the swarming Pokémon, and a 60% chance that their encounter is taken from the normal encounter table.

This swarm can only be encountered outside of winter (April, August, and December).
