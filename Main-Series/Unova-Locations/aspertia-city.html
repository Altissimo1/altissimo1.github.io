Aspertia City

Access to:
North: Route 19

Places of interest:
The player character's mother will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.
Poké Mart
Trainers' School
Aspertia Gym: The first Gym. It specializes in Normal-type Pokémon.

Utility:
Surf is required to access TM53 Energy Ball. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 16 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 1 level lower in Easy Mode and 1 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
