Clay Tunnel

Access to:
North: Twist Mountain
West: Mistralton Cave
South: Driftveil City
Accessible in the area: Underground Ruins

Utility:
Strength is required to access a Rare Bone, an HP Up, and a Nugget.
Surf is required to access a Steel Gem and an Iron, as well as six spots for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Front: Cave map with 11 spots.
Middle: Cave map with 18 spots. Four spots require Surf.
Back: Cave map with 20 spots. Four spots require riding mine carts to reach; two of those also require Surf.
All other areas: none.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Lairon and Nosepass have a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Steelix have a 5% chance of holding a Metal Coat.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Poliwrath have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
