Giant Chasm

Access to:
South: Route 13

Utility:
Strength is required to access two Star Pieces and a Comet Shard.
Surf can be used here to encounter wild Pokémon.

After capturing Kyurem, the northern parts of the caves become inaccessible.

Access to:
West: Route 23
South: Route 13
East: Route 22
Occasionally accessible from the area: Plasma Frigate

Utility:
Strength is required to access a Star Piece, a Max Repel, and an Ice Gem.
Surf is required to access TM13 Ice Beam. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Main cave: Cave map with 20 spots.
Inner forest: Forest map with 20 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Swellow have a 5% chance of holding a Charti Berry.
  • Wild Lunatone have a 5% chance of holding a Moon Stone and a 1% chance of holding a Comet Shard (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Solrock have a 5% chance of holding a Sun Stone and a 1% chance of holding a Comet Shard (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Jynx always hold an Aspear Berry.
  • Wild Sneasel have a 50% chance of holding a Grip Claw and a 5% chance of holding a Quick Claw.
  • Wild Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Poliwrath have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
  • Wild Clefairy and Clefable have a 50% chance of holding a Leppa Berry, a 5% chance of holding a Moon Stone, and a 1% chance of holding a Comet Shard (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Ditto have a 50% chance of holding Quick Powder and a 5% chance of holding Metal Powder.
  • Wild Metang and Metagross have a 5% chance of holding a Metal Coat.
