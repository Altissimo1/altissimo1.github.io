Abyssal Ruins

Access to:
Above: Undella Bay

Utility:
Surf and Dive are required to access this area.
Flash is required to reach the third floor.
Strength is required to reach the fourth floor.

Access to:
Above: Undella Bay

Utility:
Surf and Dive are required to access this area.
Flash is required to reach the third floor.
Strength is required to reach the fourth floor.

The ruins are shaped like a pyramid and have four floors. Every time the player enters the ruins, they can only take a maximum of 500 steps before being sent back to Undella Bay. Warnings are given (via sounds) at 100, 300, and 450 steps. It is impossible to get all items in one trip.

To reach the second floor, the player must reach the pillar in the middle of the first floor in 190 or fewer steps.

To reach the third floor, the player must use Flash at the purple pillar in the center-west path on the second floor.

To reach the fourth floor, the player must use Strength at the purple pillar in the center-east part of the third floor.

The treasures found here can be sold to the billionaire in Undella Town.
