Castelia Sewers

Access to:
Exits: Castelia City
Accessible in the area: Relic Passage

Places of interest:
The first time the player explores the area, Hugh will request to accompany them. Every wild battle that the player engages in while he is present will be a Double Battle, and battles against Team Plasma Grunts are also Double Battles. He has a level 20 2nd-stage starter whose type is super-effective against the player's starter, holding its appropriate type-boosting item, and a level 18 Pidove.

Utility:
During the spring and summer, the sewers are flooded and Surf is required to access much of the sewers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Pokémon

  • Wild Grimer have a 5% chance of holding Black Sludge.
  • Wild Muk have a 50% chance of holding Black Sludge and a 1% chance of holding a Toxic Orb.

Scientists

A female Scientist in a room near Doctor Heath and a male Scientist in a room near Janitor Brady will give the player one item per day, taken randomly from the below list. The male scientist requires Surf to access and can only be accessed in the spring and summer.
