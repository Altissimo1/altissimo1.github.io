Virbank Complex

Access to:
North: Virbank City

Utility:
Cut is required to access a Super Potion.
Surf can be used here to encounter wild Pokémon.

Pokémon

  • Wild Magnemite have a 5% chance of holding a Metal Coat.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Finneon and Lumineon have a 5% chance of holding a Rindo Berry.
  • Wild Qwilfish have a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
  • Wild Growlithe have a 50% chance of holding a Rawst Berry.
  • Wild Koffing have a 5% chance of holding a Smoke Ball.
