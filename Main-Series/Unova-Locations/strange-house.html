Strange House

Access to:
Exit: Reversal Mountain

Funfest Missions:
1F, left side room: Town map with 20 spots. Only accessible by making one left-to-right trip through the basement.
2F, right side room: Town map with 20 spots. Only accessible by making two left-to-right trips through the basement.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Banette have a 5% chance of holding a Spell Tag.
  • Wild Gothita, Gothorita, Solosis, and Duosion have a 5% chance of holding a Persim Berry.
