Marine Tube

Access to:
North: Humilau City
South: Undella Town

Places of interest:
A man in an orange shirt appears here if he has been given a Fresh Water on Skyarrow Bridge, Driftveil Drawbridge, Tubeline Bridge, Village Bridge, and Marvelous Bridge. If he is given a Fresh Water, he will move to Skyarrow Bridge.
