Pokémon World Tournament

Access to:
Exit: Driftveil City
Accessible in the area: Relic Passage
Occasionally accessible from the area: Plasma Frigate

Places of interest:
Exchange Service Corner
Move Reminder: In exchange for a Heart Scale, the Move Reminder can teach a Pokémon a move that it was capable of learning at a lower level than its current level, but which is not currently in its moveset.
Move Deleter
Move Tutors: Two Move Tutors will teach moves to any fully-evolved starter Pokémon with maximum friendship. One teaches Frenzy Plant, Blast Burn, and Hydro Cannon, and the other teaches Grass Pledge, Fire Pledge, and Water Pledge.

Overview

The Pokémon World Tournament is a battle facility in Black 2 and White 2. Here, the player can participate in several different types of tournaments in different battle modes.

Battles conducted in the PWT are subject to certain restrictions, shown below:

  • All Pokémon are set to level 25 in the Driftveil Tournament and to level 50 in the Rental and Mix Tournaments.
  • Opponents' Pokémon always have a fixed IV value in every stat, determined by the Trainer. The IVs of each Trainer are listed on the tournament pages.
  • Each opponent leads with a specific Pokémon, which is listed first on the tournament pages.
  • Any Pokémon holding a Soul Dew cannot enter.
  • Some Pokémon cannot be entered in any tournament (except some Download tournaments) and will never be encountered on an opponent's team:
Mewtwo Mew Lugia Ho-Oh Celebi Kyogre

Tournaments

Tournaments are chosen from two attendants at the front desk. The left attendant handles the Driftveil, Rental, Mix, and Download Tournaments, and the right attendant handles the remaining specialty tournaments. The linked tournaments have pages listing every Trainer and the Pokémon they can use.

Mix Master Win the Driftveil, Mix, and all five regional Leaders Tournaments. Uses the same rules as the Mix Tournament, though with stricter restrictions on the player's Pokémon, and only the Unova Gym Leaders from the Unova Leaders Tournament are faced, using their World Leaders Tournament teams.

Download Downloaded via Nintendo Wi-Fi Connection (discontinued May 20, 2014). Special downloadable tournaments with their own rules.
Details about the entrants in these tournaments can be found on Bulbapedia.

Battle Points

The player receives the following amounts of BP for winning a tournament. In the World Leaders and Champions Tournaments, the player instead receives 1 BP for every Pokémon that did not faint during the entire tournament.

The player will receive a Red, Yellow, Green, or Blue Shard for losing a tournament.

Trainers

The first time the player approaches this area, they will be forced to enter the Driftveil Tournament. During this tournament, the player will always battle Hugh, Cheren, and Colress, and though their Pokémon are set to level 25, the player's Pokémon will not have their level changed. After first beating this tournament, all future attempts at the tournament will use the Pokémon and Trainers specified on the Driftveil and Mix Tournament page.
