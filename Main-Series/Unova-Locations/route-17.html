Access to:
West: Route 18
East: Route 1
Accessible on the route: P2 Laboratory

Utility:
Surf is required to traverse the route, including all 11 spots for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Forest map with 11 spots. Requires Surf.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Finneon and Lumineon have a 5% chance of holding a Rindo Berry.
  • Wild Horsea, Seadra, and Kingdra have a 5% chance of holding a Dragon Scale.
  • Wild Qwilfish have a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
