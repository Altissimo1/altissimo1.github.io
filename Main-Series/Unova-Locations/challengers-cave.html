Challenger's Cave

Access to:
Exit: Route 9

Utility:
Surf is required to access most of B2F. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Pokémon

  • Wild Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Graveler have a 5% chance of holding an Everstone.
  • Wild Lickitung have a 5% chance of holding a Lagging Tail.
  • Wild Mawile have a 5% chance of holding an Occa Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Poliwrath have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
