Overview

The Big Stadium is a sports arena in Nimbasa City. Every day, a different sports event is played here, causing the stadium to be closed for the day while the sports event is taking place. The associated days, times, and sports events are shown below.

Outside of the times listed above, the player can enter the stadium and battle Trainers inside. The Trainers that are available depend on the sport being played and the number of Badges the player has at the time.
