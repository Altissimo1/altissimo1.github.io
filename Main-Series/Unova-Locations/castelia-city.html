To obtain this Zorua, the player must have received Celebi in an event for HeartGold or SoulSilver between 2010 and 2011 and transferred it to Black or White using the Relocator menu option, rather than Poké Transfer. The player has to unlock the Relocator by speaking to the Scientist on 11F of the building to the west of Central Plaza and providing one of the following phrases:
