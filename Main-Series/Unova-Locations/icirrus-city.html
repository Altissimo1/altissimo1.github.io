Icirrus City

Access to:
North: Dragonspiral Tower
East: Route 8
Accessible in the city: Twist Mountain

Places of interest:
Pokémon Fan Club
Poké Mart
Icirrus Gym: The seventh Gym. It specializes in Ice-types.
Standing near the four dancing people west of the Pokémon Center will add a clapping effect to the background music.

Utility:
Surf can be used to encounter wild Pokémon.

Access to:
North: Dragonspiral Tower
East: Route 8
Accessible in the city: Twist Mountain

Places of interest:
Pokémon Fan Club
Poké Mart
Standing near the four dancing people west of the Pokémon Center will add a clapping effect to the background music.
Speaking to a sound designer in the ex-Magma/Aqua Grunts' house will change the background music to that of Route 10 (from Black and White) as long as the player remains in the house.

Utility:
Surf can be used to encounter wild Pokémon.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 18 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Palpitoad and Seismitoad have a 50% chance of holding a Persim Berry.
  • Wild Stunfisk have a 5% chance of holding a Soft Sand.

These encounters are only available during Spring, Summer, and Autumn.
