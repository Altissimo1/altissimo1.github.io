Reversal Mountain

Access to:
West: Lentimas Town
East: Undella Town
Accessible in the area: Strange House

Places of interest:
The first time the player explores the inner area, Bianca will request to accompany them. Every wild and Trainer battle that the player engages in while she is present will be a Double Battle. She has a level 40 Musharna, a level 38 Watchog, and a level 38 Stoutland.

Pokémon

  • Wild Skorupi have a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
  • Wild Drifblim have a 1% chance of holding an Air Balloon (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Trapinch have a 5% chance of holding Soft Sand.
  • Wild Spoink and Grumpig have a 50% chance of holding a Persim Berry.
  • Wild Numel and Camerupt always hold a Rawst Berry.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Cacturne have a 5% chance of holding a Sticky Barb.
  • Wild Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
