Driftveil City

Access to:
West: Route 6
South: Cold Storage
East: Driftveil Drawbridge

Places of interest:
Driftveil Market
Team Plasma Safehouse
Move Tutor: A man in the house to the east of Driftveil Market can teach a starter Pokémon with high friendship Grass Pledge (Grass-types), Fire Pledge (Fire-types), or Water Pledge (Water-types).
Driftveil Gym: The fifth Gym. It specializes in Ground-types.

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Water Stone. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
West: Route 6
South: Pokémon World Tournament
East: Driftveil Drawbridge
Accessible from the city: Clay Tunnel

Places of interest:
Driftveil Market
Team Plasma Safehouse
Move Tutor
Driftveil Gym: The fifth Gym. It specializes in Ground-types.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 20 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 3 levels lower in Easy Mode and 3 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Chinchou and Lanturn have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Luvdisc have a 50% chance of holding a Heart Scale.
