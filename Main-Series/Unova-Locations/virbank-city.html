Virbank City

Access to:
North: Pokéstar Studios
West: Route 20
South: Virbank Complex
Accessible via the ship at the harbor: Castelia City

Places of interest:
Poké Mart
Virbank Gym: The second Gym. It specializes in Poison-types.

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Rare Candy. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Pokémon

  • Wild Finneon and Lumineon have a 5% chance of holding a Rindo Berry.
  • Wild Qwilfish have a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
