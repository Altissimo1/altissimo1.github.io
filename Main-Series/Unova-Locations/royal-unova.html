Trainers

The number of Trainers on board depends on the day of the week. A crew member will alert the player to this number and will remind the player how many Trainers they have already battled when spoken to. Two crew members, one on the left and one on the right, will provide hints as to the location of Trainers.
The player has only 3 minutes to find all the Trainers.
