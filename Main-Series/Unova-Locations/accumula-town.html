Access to:

South: Route 1

Places of interest:

Speaking to the pianist and the drummer in the northeastern house, and showing the pianist a Kricketot and a Whismur, adds piano and percussion to the background music.

Town map with 13 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.
