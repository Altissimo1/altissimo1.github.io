Relic Passage

Access to:
South: Castelia Sewers
East: Pokémon World Tournament
Northeast: Relic Castle

Utility:
Strength is required to access a Rock Gem, an Ultra Ball, a Rare Candy, and a Rocky Helmet.
Surf is required to access a Big Pearl. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Castelia side: Cave map with 19 spots. 15 spots are inaccessible when entering from Castelia Sewers. Funfest Trainers: ±2 levels.
Central area: Cave map with 20 spots. One spot is in an enclosed room that requires going to the Castelia side and back. Funfest Trainers: ±3 levels.
Driftveil/PWT side: Cave map with 19 spots. Funfest Trainers: ±3 levels.
All other areas: none.
Funfest Trainer levels are lowered by the listed amount in Easy Mode and raised by it in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Roggenrola and Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Poliwrath have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
