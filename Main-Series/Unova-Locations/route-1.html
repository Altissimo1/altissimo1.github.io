Route 1

Access to:
North: Accumula Town
West: Route 17
South: Nuvema Town

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Max Ether, a Big Mushroom, a Pearl, and several Trainers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
North: Accumula Town
West: Route 17
South: Nuvema Town

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Full Restore, a Prism Scale, a Protein, and several Trainers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Pokémon

  • Wild Scraggy have a 5% chance of holding a Shed Shell.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Farfetch'd have a 5% chance of holding a Stick.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
