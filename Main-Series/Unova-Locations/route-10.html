Route 10

Access to:
South: Opelucid City
Accessible on the route: Victory Road

Pokémon

  • Wild Foongus and Amoonguss have a 50% chance of holding a TinyMushroom, a 5% chance of holding a Big Mushroom, and a 1% chance of holding a BalmMushroom (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Sawk and Throh have a 5% chance of holding a Black Belt and a 1% chance of holding an Expert Belt (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
