Relic Castle

Access to:
Exit on 1F: Desert Resort

Only the first two floors are accessible before N awakens a dragon at Dragonspiral Tower. Part of the lowest floor is only accessible after defeating the Elite Four.

Access to:
Exit on 1F: Desert Resort
Accessible in the area: Relic Passage

Funfest Missions:
1F: Cave map with 14 spots. 6 of the spots require falling into a quicksand pit and coming back up from the basement to reach. Funfest Trainers: ±2 levels.
B1: Cave map with 20 spots. 9 of the spots are only accessible from the southern staircase. The other 11 spots are only accessible from quicksand pits. Funfest Trainers: ±2 levels.
B7, far right room: Cave map with 12 spots. Only accessible from Relic Passage, and only by entering from the Driftveil/PWT side. Funfest Trainers: ±3 levels.
B7, center room: Special-High map with 3 spots. Only accessible from Relic Passage, and only by entering from the Driftveil/PWT side. Funfest Trainers: ±3 levels.
B7, far left room: Special-Low map with 12 spots. Only accessible from Relic Passage, and only by entering from the Driftveil/PWT side. Funfest Trainers: ±3 levels.
All other areas: none.
Funfest Trainer levels are lowered by the listed amount in Easy Mode and raised by it in Challenge Mode.
