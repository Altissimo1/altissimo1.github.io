Route 14

Access to:
North: Undella Town
South: Black City (Black) or White Forest (White)
Accessible on the route: Abundant Shrine

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Max Elixir, an Ultra Ball, a Reaper Cloth, TM08 Bulk Up, and two Trainers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Waterfall is required to access an Ultra Ball, a Reaper Cloth, TM08 Bulk Up, and two Trainers.

Access to:
North: Undella Town
South: Black City (Black 2) or White Forest (White 2)
Accessible on the route: Abundant Shrine

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Heart Scale, a Protein, two Nuggets, and two Trainers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Waterfall is required to access a Protein, two Nuggets, and Black Belt Jay.

Pokémon

  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Yanma have a 5% chance of holding a Wide Lens.
  • Wild Buizel and Floatzel have a 5% chance of holding a Wacan Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.

Swarm
