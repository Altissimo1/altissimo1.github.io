Pokémon

If the player collects the Dropped Item and returns it to its owner (Curtis if the player character is female and Yancy if the player character is male), and then calls the owner 15 times, the owner will start offering to trade the player Pokémon. The player can trade one Pokémon per day in the order listed below. Once all Pokémon have been received, the cycle repeats indefinitely.

The player can trade any Pokémon in these trades. All received Pokémon are level 50 and have their Hidden Ability.

In-Game Trade - Curtis

These trades are used if the player character is female.
