Route 16

Access to:
West: Nimbasa City
East: Marvelous Bridge
Accessible on the route: Lostlorn Forest

Utility:
Cut is required to access a Rare Candy.
Strength is required to access TM66 Payback, a Charcoal, Backpacker Lora, and the dark grass patches.

Access to:
West: Nimbasa City
East: Marvelous Bridge
Accessible on the route: Lostlorn Forest

Utility:
Cut is required to access a Heal Ball.
Strength is required to access TM66 Payback, a TinyMushroom, Backpacker Blossom, and the dark grass patches.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 12 spots. 2 of the spots require using Cut to reach.
Funfest Trainers here are 2 levels lower in Easy Mode and 2 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Minccino and Cinccino have a 50% chance of holding a Chesto Berry.
  • Wild Trubbish have a 5% chance of holding Black Sludge and a 1% chance of holding a Nugget (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
