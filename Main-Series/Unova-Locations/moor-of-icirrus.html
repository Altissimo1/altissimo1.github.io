Moor of Icirrus

Access to:
South: Route 8

Places of interest:
If a Keldeo obtained in a fateful encounter, Cobalion, Terrakion, and Virizion are brought here, Keldeo can learn Secret Sword.

Utility:
Strength is required to access an Ultra Ball, a Max Ether, a Carbos, and Parasol Lady Mariah.
Surf can be used here to encounter wild Pokémon.

Access to:
South: Route 8

Utility:
Strength is required to access TM84 Poison Jab and Pokémon Ranger Elaine.
Surf is required to access a Green Shard. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
