Striaton City

Access to:
North: Route 3
South: Route 2
East: Dreamyard

Places of interest:
Trainers' School
Fennel's House
Poké Mart
Striaton Gym: The first Gym. It specializes in Normal-types and in the type of Grass, Fire, or Water that the player's starter is weak to.

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Zinc and a Big Pearl. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
North: Route 3
South: Route 2
East: Dreamyard

Places of interest:
Trainers' School
Poké Mart
Striaton Restaurant

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Big Pearl and a PP Up, as well as one spot for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 18 spots. One spot requires Surf.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
