Floccesy Ranch

Access to:
South: Route 20

Places of interest:
The ranch owner's wife will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.
Utility:
Surf is required to access the Hidden Grotto. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Forest map with 20 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 1 level lower in Easy Mode and 1 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Politoed have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.

Walking

Walking (rustling grass)
