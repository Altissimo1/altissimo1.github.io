Route 23

Access to:
North: Victory Road
Accessible on the route: Giant Chasm

Utility:
Cut or Surf is required to access a Heart Scale, as well as one spot for Funfest Missions.
Cut is required to access a Full Heal, an Ultra Ball, a Repeat Ball, an HP Up, a Calcium, and several Trainers.
Cut and Strength are required to access a Yellow Shard, a Green Shard, a Max Potion, a PP Up, TM12 Taunt, and Battle Girl Tiffany.
Surf is required to access TM35 Flamethrower, as well as seven spots for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Forest map with 20 spots. Seven spots require Surf. 1 other spot requires Surf or Cut.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Amoonguss have a 50% chance of holding a TinyMushroom, a 5% chance of holding a Big Mushroom, and a 1% chance of holding a BalmMushroom (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Sawk and Throh have a 5% chance of holding a Black Belt and a 1% chance of holding an Expert Belt (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Zangoose have a 5% chance of holding a Quick Claw.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Politoed have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
