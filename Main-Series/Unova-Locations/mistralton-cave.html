Mistralton Cave

Access to:
Exit: Route 6

Utility:
Surf is required to access Mistralton Cave.
Strength is required to access an Elixir, two Hyper Potions, and a Max Repel.

Access to:
Exit: Route 6
Accessible from the area: Clay Tunnel

Utility:
