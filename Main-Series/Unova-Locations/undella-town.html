Funfest Missions:
Town map with 20 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Corsola have a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Shellder and Cloyster have a 50% chance of holding a Pearl and a 5% chance of holding a Big Pearl.
  • Wild Luvdisc have a 50% chance of holding a Heart Scale.

The Riches

The player can only fight the Riches once per day. The first day, speaking to Draco will trigger a battle with Draco. The next day, speaking to Draco will trigger a battle with Draco, followed by a battle with Susan. This pattern continues until the player defeats Miles, at which point the player cannot battle the Riches any longer.
