Dragonspiral Tower

Access to:
South: Icirrus City

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Heart Scale, TM63 Embargo, and two Trainers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
South: Icirrus City

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Carbos. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Pokémon

  • Wild Cubchoo and Beartic have a 50% chance of holding an Aspear Berry.
  • Wild Druddigon have a 5% chance of holding a Dragon Fang.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding an Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Dratini, Dragonair, and Dragonite have a 5% chance of holding a Dragon Scale.
  • Wild Golett have a 5% chance of holding a Light Clay.
