Route 6

Access to:
East: Driftveil City
Accessible on the route: Chargestone Cave
Accessible on the route: Mistralton Cave

Places of interest:
A woman in the house north of the research lab will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.

Utility:
Surf is required to access TM84 Poison Jab, a Silk Scarf, two TinyMushrooms, a Big Mushroom, and the entrance to Mistralton Cave. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
East: Driftveil City
Accessible on the route: Chargestone Cave
Accessible on the route: Mistralton Cave

Places of interest:
A woman in the house north of the research lab will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.

Utility:
Surf is required to access TM56 Fling, a Heart Scale, a PP Up, a Big Mushroom, and the entrance to Mistralton Cave. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 15 spots. 4 of the spots require using Surf to reach.
Funfest Trainers here are 3 levels lower in Easy Mode and 3 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Foongus have a 50% chance of holding a TinyMushroom, a 5% chance of holding a Big Mushroom, and a 1% chance of holding a BalmMushroom (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Swadloon and Leavanny have a 5% chance of holding a Mental Herb.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry and a 5% chance of holding an Oran Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Politoed have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.
