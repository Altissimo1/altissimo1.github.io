Village Bridge

Access to:
West: Route 11
East: Route 12

Places of interest:
Village Bridge Restaurant: A minigame can be played here.
The woman in the easternmost house will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.
When the player enters the house of the woman with four Patrat, she and her Patrat will move to Marvelous Bridge.
Speaking to the musicians adds grass flute, acoustic guitar, beatboxing, and enka vocal parts to the background music.

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Calcium and an Ultra Ball. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
West: Route 11
East: Route 12

Places of interest:
The woman in the easternmost house will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.
When the player enters the house of the woman with four Patrat, she and her Patrat will move to Marvelous Bridge.
Speaking to the musicians adds grass flute, acoustic guitar, beatboxing, and enka vocal parts to the background music.
A man in an orange shirt appears here if he has been given a Fresh Water on Skyarrow Bridge, Driftveil Drawbridge, and Tubeline Bridge. If he is given a Fresh Water, he will move to Marvelous Bridge.

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Big Pearl and a Heart Scale, as well as seven spots for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 15 spots. Seven spots require Surf.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Bibarel and Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Zangoose have a 5% chance of holding a Quick Claw.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin, Carvanha, and Sharpedo have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
