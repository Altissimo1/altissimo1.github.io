Anville Town

Access to:
Accessible via the Anville Line at the subway station: Nimbasa City

Places of interest:
Being near the flute player on the bridge overlooking the turntable will add flute to the background music.

Access to:
Accessible via the Anville Line at the subway station: Nimbasa City

Places of interest:
Being near the flute player on the bridge overlooking the turntable will add flute to the background music.

Funfest Missions:
Town map with 18 spots. Only accessible from the Battle Subway's brown line, and cannot be flown to.
Funfest Trainers here are 2 levels lower in Easy Mode and 2 higher in Challenge Mode.

Items

Black and White Items
Item Location Notes
Ultra Ball In the grass in the north part of the town Hidden.
Rare Candy Obtained from a Depot Agent in the southeastern house

Lost-and-found

The Depot Agent in the southeastern house will give the player an item every time they win seven consecutive battles in the Battle Subway. The higher the player's overall streak is, the more of each item he will give out, to a maximum of ten. The possible items he can give are shown below.

Rare Candy Full Restore Max Revive Elixir Protein
Iron Calcium Zinc Carbos HP Up

Trades

Every Saturday and Sunday, except for the 8th, 18th, or 28th day of the month, traders in town will trade their items for items from the player. The possible trades are shown below.

Black and White Items (Traders)
Player's Item(s) Received Item
2x Escape Rope Revive
1x Star Piece PP Up
20x Poké Ball Full Restore
