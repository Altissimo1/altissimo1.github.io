Wellspring Cave

Access to:
Exit on 1F: Route 3

Utility:
Surf is required to access most of 1F and all of B1F. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Flash is recommended on B1F.

Access to:
Exit on 1F: Route 3

Utility:
Surf is required to access most of 1F and all of B1F, including 15 spots for Funfest Missions (10 on 1F and all five on B1F). Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Flash is recommended on B1F.

Funfest Missions:
1F: Cave map with 14 spots. 10 spots require Surf.
B1F: Special-High map with 5 spots. Requires Surf and Flash.
All other areas: none.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Roggenrola have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Poliwrath have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.

Memory Link encounter

After viewing the Memory Link flashback "Meeting Friends, Saying Good-Bye", the player may encounter N's Woobat here. The appearance rate depends on how many of N's Pokémon the player has already captured, as shown below.

  • 0 captured: 40%
  • 1-5 captured: 20%
  • 6-11 captured: 10%
  • 12+ captured: 5%
