Victory Road

Access to:
North: Pokémon League
South: Route 10

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Max Revive and a Rare Candy. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
West: Pokémon League
South: Route 23
Accessible in the area: N's Castle

Utility:
Strength is required to traverse the area.
Cut is required to access a Full Restore.
Surf is required to access an Escape Rope, TM93 Wild Charge, a Max Elixir, a Rare Candy, and some Trainers. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Waterfall is required to access TM93 Wild Charge and some Trainers.
Flash is recommended.

Pokémon

  • Wild Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
  • Wild Poliwhirl and Poliwrath have a 5% chance of holding a King's Rock.

  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.

  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Boldore have a 50% chance of holding an Everstone and a 5% chance of holding a Hard Stone.
  • Wild Druddigon have a 5% chance of holding a Dragon Fang.
  • Wild Throh and Sawk have a 5% chance of holding a Black Belt and a 1% chance of holding an Expert Belt (in dark grass only).
