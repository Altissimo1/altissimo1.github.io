Cave of Being

Access to:
Exit: Route 20

Places of interest:
If the player comes here after defeating the Elite Four and speaks to Professor Juniper, then Uxie, Mesprit, and Azelf will appear and travel to Nacrene City, Celestial Tower, and Route 23, respectively.

Utility:
Surf is required to access this area.
