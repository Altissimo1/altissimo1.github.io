Lostlorn Forest

Access to:
South: Route 16

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Rare Candy and a Protein. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Waterfall is required to access a Rare Candy and a Protein.

Access to:
South: Route 16

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Rare Candy, a Grass Gem, and Veteran Murphy, as well as five spots for Funfest Missions. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.
Waterfall is required to access a Rare Candy, a Grass Gem, and Veteran Murphy, as well as five spots for Funfest Missions.

Funfest Missions:
Forest map with 20 spots. Five spots require Surf and Waterfall.
Funfest Trainers here are 2 levels lower in Easy Mode and 2 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Swadloon and Leavanny have a 5% chance of holding a Mental Herb.
  • Wild Venipede have a 50% chance of holding a Pecha Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Panpour have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Rindo Berry.
  • Wild Pansage have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding an Occa Berry.
  • Wild Pansear have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Passho Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
