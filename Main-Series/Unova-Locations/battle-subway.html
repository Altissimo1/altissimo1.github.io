The Battle Subway is a facility, accessible in Black, White, Black 2, and White 2, where the goal is to defeat Trainers without losing in one of three different modes. Each mode has a leader, known as the Subway Boss, who can be battled after a given number of Trainers have been defeated, without the player losing to any one. Each Subway Boss has two encounters in their mode. Rewards can be obtained from people on the platforms for achieving specific win milestones as well; information about these items is given below.

The subway station that contains the Battle Subway also contains the Anville Train to take the player to Anville Town.

Battles conducted in the Subway are subject to restrictions.

  • The player will not receive money, and their Pokémon will not receive experience. Instead, the player will receive BP, which is used as a currency for purchasable items in the Battle Maison.
  • Enemy Pokémon, and Pokémon used by allied Trainers in Multi Battles, are always level 50. The player's Pokémon above or below level 50 will be set to level 50.
  • Set Mode is enforced.
  • Items cannot be used in battle.
  • The moves Return and Frustration, when used by the opponent, will always have their maximum power.
  • Losing a battle will reset the player's streak to 0. Turning off the game mid-battle will count as a loss and reset the streak.
  • Pokémon encountered in battles will not be added to the Pokédex.
  • Enemy Pokémon will always take their Nature, Item, moves, and EVs from predetermined 'sets'.
  • Enemy Pokémon that can have multiple Abilities will always have a random Ability, although they cannot have their Hidden Ability. Enemy Pokémon that can be male or female will have their gender randomly determined.
  • The player cannot enter two Pokémon of the same species or holding the same held item, unless they are playing Multis.
  • Any Pokémon holding a Soul Dew cannot enter.
  • Some Pokémon cannot be entered altogether, and will never be encountered on an opponent's team:

Subway Bosses

Each of the two regular modes (Singles and Doubles) has a single Subway Boss that serves as the leader of that mode. The player will battle them first as the 21th battle on the Normal Train and again as the 49th battle on the Super Train, and a commemorative monument will be placed in the player's room for each Subway Boss the player defeats on the Super Train. In Black 2 and White 2, obtained monuments from Black and White can be seen in the player's room if they use Memory Link with that game. The Subway Bosses' teams are always the same, but the order of Pokémon within their party varies.

When battling a Subway Boss in Multis, the player will engage both Subway Bosses in a Multi Battle.

Their Pokémon always have 15 IVs on the Normal Train and 31 on the Super Train.
