Route 3

Access to:
West: Nacrene City
South: Striaton City
Accessible on the route: Wellspring Cave

Utility:
Surf is required to access a Full Heal, a Rare Candy, a Max Ether, an HP Up, and Pokémon Breeder Galen. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
West: Nacrene City
South: Striaton City
Accessible on the route: Wellspring Cave

Utility:
Surf is required to access an Ultra Ball, a Calcium, a Full Restore, and Fisherman Mack. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 20 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Blitzle have a 50% chance of holding a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
