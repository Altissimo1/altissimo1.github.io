Memory Link encounters

After viewing the Memory Link flashback "Meeting Friends, Saying Good-Bye", the player may encounter N's Boldore, Joltik, Ferroseed, and Klink here. Their appearance rates depend on how many of N's Pokémon the player has already captured, as shown below.

  • 0 captured: 40%
  • 1-5 captured: 20%
  • 6-11 captured: 10%
  • 12+ captured: 5%
