Places of interest:
A person in the westernmost (Black 2) or easternmost (White 2) house on the street will heal the player's Pokémon when spoken to.

Utility:
Surf can be used here to encounter wild Pokémon.

Funfest Missions:
Black 2 only: Road map with 12 spots.
White 2 only: Road map with 12 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 2 levels lower in Easy Mode and 2 higher in Challenge Mode.
