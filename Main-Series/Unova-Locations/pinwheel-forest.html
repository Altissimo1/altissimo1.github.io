Pinwheel Forest

Access to:
North: Skyarrow Bridge
East: Nacrene City

Utility:
Surf is required to access SilverPowder. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Access to:
North: Skyarrow Bridge
East: Nacrene City

Places of interest:
The first time the player explores the southwestern area, Cheren will request to accompany them. Every wild and Trainer battle that the player engages in while he is present will be a Double Battle. He has a level 67 Stoutland, a level 65 Watchog, and a level 65 Cinccino.

Utility:
Surf is required to access a PP Max. Wild Pokémon can also be encountered here with Surf.

Funfest Missions:
Outer: Forest map with 17 spots.
Inner: Forest map with 20 spots. 1 of the spots requires using Surf to reach.
All other areas: none.
Funfest Trainers here are 5 levels lower in Easy Mode and 5 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Tympole have a 50% chance of holding a Persim Berry.
  • Wild Sawk and Throh have a 5% chance of holding a Black Belt and a 1% chance of holding an Expert Belt (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Sewaddle and Swadloon have a 5% chance of holding a Mental Herb.
  • Wild Venipede and Whirlipede have a 50% chance of holding a Pecha Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Poison Barb.
  • Wild Panpour have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Rindo Berry.
  • Wild Pansage have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding an Occa Berry.
  • Wild Pansear have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Passho Berry.
  • Wild Red-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaTooth.
  • Wild Blue-Striped Basculin have a 5% chance of holding a DeepSeaScale.
