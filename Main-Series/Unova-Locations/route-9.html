Route 9

Access to:
West: Tubeline Bridge
East: Opelucid City
Accessible on the route: Challenger's Cave

Places of interest:
Shopping Mall Nine: The storage room contains appliances for Rotom to use to change form.

Access to:
West: Tubeline Bridge
East: Opelucid City

Places of interest:
Shopping Mall Nine: The storage room contains appliances for Rotom to use to change form.

Funfest Missions:
Road map with 12 spots.
Funfest Trainers here are 4 levels lower in Easy Mode and 4 higher in Challenge Mode.

Pokémon

  • Wild Garbodor have a 50% chance of holding Black Sludge, a 5% chance of holding a Nugget, and a 1% chance of holding a Big Nugget (in dark grass only).
  • Wild Minccino and Cinccino have a 50% chance of holding a Chesto Berry.
  • Wild Audino have a 50% chance of holding an Oran Berry and a 5% chance of holding a Sitrus Berry.
  • Wild Emolga always hold a Cheri Berry.
  • Wild Poochyena have a 5% chance of holding a Pecha Berry.
