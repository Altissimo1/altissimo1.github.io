Obstacles

  • A Dead Tree requires Tackle x3 to clear, which can be found on Piloswine.
  • A Rock Wall requires Crush x3 to clear, which can be found on nearby Golem. It leads to a wild Kangaskhan.
  • Two Posts require Cross to cross, which can be found on Tangela.
  • A Rockfall requires Crush x3 to clear, which can be found on nearby Golem and Kangaskhan.
  • A Vine requires Soak x1 to clear, which can be found on nearby Poliwhirl.
  • A Rock Wall requires Crush x3 to clear, which can be found on nearby Golem and kangaskhan. This wall, the previous rockfall, and the vine all lead to wild Gastly.
  • An Ice Block requires Burn x3 to clear. This ice block, the previous rock wall, the rockfall, and the vine all lead to Regirock.
  • Huge Flowers require Gust x3 to clear, which can be found on nearby Altaria. They are wild Vileplume.
