Professor Oak Challenge

A Professor Oak challenge is where you catch all available Pokémon before moving on to the next story event. I have not found a guide for this game, so this page may be the first. Please contact me if you know of an existing guide or know of inaccuracies in this guide.

A total of 307 Pokémon can be obtained in this game, including the six accessible in Ranger Net missions. Without access to those events, the total available Pokémon number 301.

Part of the way through the Sky Fortress, the player will encounter a Magmortar. At this point, there is an Ice Block in Mt. Sorbet that requires three Pokémon with Burn x3 to clear. The player has access to a Camerupt at Faldera Volcano already, and the wild Magmortar in the Sky Fortress is a second Pokémon. I believe it should be possible to complete the following steps:

  1. Leave the Sky Fortress.
  2. Get far enough in Past Missions to clear Mission 18: Panic at the Perplexing Temple, which triggers the removal of a door in Mt. Sorbet leading to a wild Infernape.
  3. Catch the Infernape and use Camerupt, Magmortar, and Infernape to clear the Ice Block.
  4. Capture a Salamence (276 total Pokémon) in the room behind the block

However, I have not yet tested this route. Also, it is possible to catch another Magmortar later on without needing to complete any Past Missions.
