Many Times, Anytime Capture Machine

After completing the Quest Power for Nema at Rand's house, speaking with Nema in her laboratory will allow the player to engage with the Many Times, Anytime Capture Machine. When in use, the player will see a vision of Dolce Island with several strong Pokémon on it that are available for the player to capture. All Pokémon that are fought in the Capture Arena have a much higher Friendship Gauge than normal and tend to attack more frequently, making them more difficult to capture. However, the Many Times, Anytime Capture Machine is the only place where the player can find certain Pokémon after they are caught during the story, meaning that a player wishing to get a higher capture rank on a Pokémon will need to use the this feature to encounter them.

The player can bring Pokémon to use their Poké Assists; however, any Pokémon captured while using the capture machine cannot be used for their Poké Assist.
