Obstacles

  • A Mechanical Wall requires Crush x4 to clear, which can be found on nearby Drapion.
  • An Electric Fence requires Electrify x3 to clear, which can be found on nearby Manectric and Electivire.
  • A Metal Fence requires Cut x3 to clear, which can be found on Gliscor and Weavile.

Quest: Electivire Visions

The Old Man in the Aqua Resort requests to see an Electivire. Electivire can be found on Mt. Layuda.
