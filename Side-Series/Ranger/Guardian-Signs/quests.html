Quests

This page contains information about the Quests that can be taken in Guardian Signs, including the location, requirements, and rewards for each quest. Use the box below to jump to a particular quest.

The Quests here are ordered according to when they appear to be taken by the player.



Pichu's Broken Ukulele

This Quest can be received from Booker in his house in Cocona Village after completing Mission 1: Drive the Pinchers out of the Woods!. The player must complete this Quest to progress the game.

To complete this Quest, the player must go to Dolce Island and investigate the large hollow tree.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

Recharge Pachirisu

This Quest can be received from a Little Girl next to the northeastern house in Cocona Village after capturing Celebi in Cocona Plaza.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Pachirisu and bring it to the Little Girl. Pachirisu can be found on Lapras Beach, Teakwood Forest, and Rasp Cavern.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

A New Path in Teakwood Forest

This Quest can be received from an Old Man in Cocona Plaza after capturing Celebi there.

To complete this Quest, the player must clear a Wood Fence in Teakwood Forest.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

The Elderly Couple's Argument

This Quest can be received from a Man next to the southwestern house in Cocona Village after capturing Celebi in Cocona Plaza.

To complete this Quest, the player must first listen to the couple argue in the southwestern house, and then capture a a Geodude and bring it to the Man. Geodude can be found in Rasp Cavern.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

The Big One That Got Away

This Quest can be received from a Man on Lapras Beach after completing Mission 2: Salvage the Sunkern UFO!.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Luvdisc with a lure in the Coral Sea.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

The Son's Lunch

This Quest can be received from the Old Woman in the southwestern house in Cocona Village after completing Mission 3: Liberate the Wireless Tower!.

To complete this Quest, the player must take the lunch to the Young Man in the brown jacket at the Wireless Tower.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points. Completing it will automatically trigger the Quest My Lunch.

My Lunch

This Quest can be received from the Young Man in the brown jacket at the Wireless Tower after completing Mission 3: Liberate the Wireless Tower!. This Quest is obtained automatically on the conclusion of the Quest The Son's Lunch.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture Pidgey on the windy level of the Wireless Tower.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

The Sleepless Granny

This Quest can be received from the Old Woman in the house next to the cave entrance in Tilt Village after completing Mission 4: Investigate the Old Mansion!.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Noctowl and bring it to the Old Woman. Noctowl can be found outside the Old Mansion.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

Rolling Graveler

This Quest can be received from the Man east of the western entrance to Hinder Cape after learning about Signs from Leanne.

To complete this Quest, the player must go to Mt. Latolato and calm some Graveler there.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points and Graveler's Sign.

The Mild Mankey

This Quest can be received from the Lady in the southeasternmost house in Tilt Village after learning about Signs from Leanne.

To complete this Quest, the player must go to the Old Mansion and calm a Mankey there.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points and Mankey's Sign.

Cute, Cute Jumpluff

This Quest can be received from the Boy in the eastern part of Mitonga Road after completing Mission 5: Rescue Ben/Summer!

To complete this Quest, the player must go north on Mitonga Road and calm the Jumpluff there.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points and Jumpluff's Sign.

Mitonga Island's Odd Stones

This Quest can be received from Casey near the western exit to Tilt Village after completing Mission 5: Rescue Ben/Summer!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to Casey to go to Noir Forest and use Entei to clear all the rocks there.

The reward for completing this quest is 10 Ranger Points.

Luggage for Renbow Island

This Quest can be received from the Lady on the eastern side of the Big Booker Bridge after completing Mission 5: Rescue Ben/Summer!

To complete this Quest, the player must deliver her parcel to Herb in the southeastern house in Cocona Village, and then deliver his parcel back to the Lady.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points.

Fly, Starly!

This Quest can be received from the Old Man in Cocona Plaza after completing Mission 5: Rescue Ben/Summer!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to the Old Man to go to Teakwood Forest and then chase Starly toward the northern part of the forest.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points.

Save Bidoof!

This Quest can be received from the Little Boy next to the dais in Cocona Village after completing Mission 5: Rescue Ben/Summer!

To complete this Quest, the player must go to Teakwood Forest, outside the entrance to Rasp Cavern, and calm a Bidoof there.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points and Bidoof's Sign.

Trash the Pokémon Traps

This Quest can be received from the Old Woman near the western exit to Tilt Village after completing Mission 6: Foil Blue Eyes's Plans!

To complete this Quest, the player must destroy three Small Cages, which require Cut x1 to clear: set by the Pinchers. They are located in:

  1. Dangerous Cliff
  2. Old Mansion, near the pond to the east
  3. Mitonga Road, in the southeast

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points.

Search for the Runaway Boy

This Quest can be received from the Lady in Tilt Village after completing Mission 6: Foil Blue Eyes's Plans!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to her son in the Daybreak Ruins.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points.

Maddeningly Spicy!

This Quest can be received from the Girl in Curl Bay after completing Mission 6: Foil Blue Eyes's Plans!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to the Lady to go to Latolato Trail and capture an agitated Mothim, Gible, Bibarel, and Stunky.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points.

Chase the Flying Tools

This Quest can be received from the Man in the house at the base of the Wireless Tower after completing Mission 7: Protect Moltres!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Beldum near the top of the Wireless Tower.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points.

Happiny, Come Home!

This Quest can be received from the Man near Tilt Village on Mitonga Road after completing Mission 7: Protect Moltres!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Happiny in Noir Forest and return it to the Man.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Boy's Quiz

This Quest can be received from the Boy next to Booker's house in Cocona Village after learning about Suicune's appearance in the Canal Ruins.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Drifloon in the Sky, add it to their party by talking to Supurna in Aqua Resort, and bring it to the Boy.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

Spin, Hitmontop, Spin!

This Quest can be received from the Man on the winding path across the river on Sophian Road after learning about Suicune's appearance in the Canal Ruins.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture the Man's Hitmontop.

The reward for completing this quest is 15 Ranger Points and Hitmontop's Sign.

Vulpix's Ruins Exploration

This Quest can be received from the Young Man outside the entrance to the Daybreak Ruins after completing Mission 8: Appease Suicune's Wrath!

To complete this Quest, the player must calm a Vulpix inside the ruins.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points and Vulpix's Sign.

A Fisherman's Litter

This Quest can be received from the Fisherman on the northern dock in the Aqua Resort after completing Mission 8: Appease Suicune's Wrath!

To complete this Quest, the player must chase a Lumineon in a hole that opened in the Canal Ruins, then return to the Fisherman.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Photographer's Request

This Quest can be received from Yoran on the road west of Mt. Sorbet on Sophian Road after completing Mission 8: Appease Suicune's Wrath!

To complete this Quest, the player must bring Yoran a Dunsparce. Dunsparce can be found in Rasp Cavern.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

Purugly, Come Home!

This Quest can be received from the Young Lady in the northeastern house in Tilt Village after completing Mission 9: Chase After Red Eyes! and reporting to Rand about it.

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Purugly in the dining room in the Old Mansion and return it to the Young Lady.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Fisherman's Rage

This Quest can be received from the Fisherman on the cliff in the west part of the outside area of the Old Mansion after completing Mission 9: Chase After Red Eyes! and reporting to Rand about it.

To complete this Quest, the player must bring defeat three Pinchers in the part of Mitonga Road east of the exit to the Daybreak Ruins.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

A Hot Meal for Buneary

This Quest can be received from the Girl on Sophian Road south of the entrance to Mt. Sorbet after completing Mission 9: Chase After Red Eyes! and reporting to Rand about it.

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to the Girl to go to Mt. Sorbet, corral four Buneary in a dead-end, and then capture them.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points and Buneary's Sign.

Brother on the Dangerous Cliff

This Quest can be received from the Young Lady on the wooden stairs in Tilt Village after completing Mission 10: Rescue Nema and Leanne!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to the lady's brother on the Dangerous Cliff and then defeat the Pinchers that appear.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Piplup in Faldera Volcano

This Quest can be received from the Man at the entrance of Faldera Volcano after completing Mission 10: Rescue Nema and Leanne!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to the Man to go further up the volcano, then corral Piplup to the southwest.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Friendly Guide

This Quest can be received from the Old Woman in the main plaza at the Aqua Resort after completing Mission 10: Rescue Nema and Leanne!

To complete this Quest, the player must bring the Old Woman a Ludicolo. Ludicolo can be found in the Canal Ruins.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Famous Ranger Detective

This Quest can be received from the Old Man in the northeastern house in Aqua Resort after completing Mission 10: Rescue Nema and Leanne!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture the Sneasel at the docks of the resort.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Forest Vespiquen

This Quest can be received from the Lady on Sophian Road south of the entrance to Mt. Sorbet after completing Mission 10: Rescue Nema and Leanne!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture two Vespiquen north of the entrance to the Canal Ruins.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Coral Charm

This Quest can be received from Aluna on Lapras Beach after starting Mission 13: Destroy the Fortress Barrier!

To complete this Quest, the player must retrieve a Shining Coral Charm in the Coral Sea.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

Something Money Can't Buy

This Quest can be received from the Old Woman in the main plaza at the Aqua Resort after starting Mission 13: Destroy the Fortress Barrier!

To complete this Quest, the player must retrieve a parcel in the Eastern Sea and then defeat the Pinchers that appear.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

The Ninetales in Mt. Sorbet

This Quest can be received from the Old Man in the cabin on the higher slopes of Mt. Sorbet after starting Mission 13: Destroy the Fortress Barrier!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Ninetales further up on Mt. Sorbet and return it to the Old Man.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

Electivire Visions

This Quest can be received from the Old Man in the southwestern house in Aqua Resort after completing Mission 13: Destroy the Fortress Barrier!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture an Electivire on Mt. Layuda and bring it to the Old Man.

The reward for completing this quest is 20 Ranger Points.

Herb's Research

This Quest can be received from Herb in the southeastern house in Cocona Village after completing Mission 14: Protect the Peace of Oblivia!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture Vileplume, Venusaur, and Carnivine and bring them to Herb. Vileplume can be found in the Canal Ruins, Venusaur can be found on Latolato Trail, and Carnivine can be found on Sophian Road.

Note: If the player does not have Vileplume and Carnivine in the party when they complete Mission 14, they will have to collect them from Sophian Island. However, landing on Sophian Island automatically triggers the cutscene that begins Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!. Only when the player regains control after starting this Mission will they be able to move freely around Sophian Island.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

A Cry from the Sky

This Quest can be received from the Young Lady in the central area of Cocona Village after completing Mission 14: Protect the Peace of Oblivia!

To complete this Quest, the player must use Latias to fly over the area where Dolce Island was and capture a Togekiss there.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

A Fun Ride in the Sky

This Quest can be received from Nema in Rand's House after completing Mission 14: Protect the Peace of Oblivia!

To complete this Quest, the player must use speak to Nema to go to the sky with her. While there, her Z.Z. Flyer malfunctions, and the player must dodge attacks similar to those launched by Pinchers using Z.Z. Flyers earlier in the game.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

Gather the Drifloon!

This Quest can be received from the Man on the Dangerous Cliff after completing Mission 14: Protect the Peace of Oblivia!

To complete this Quest, the player must use speak to the Man to go further west on the cliff, corral four Drifloon in a dead-end, and then capture Sableye, Armaldo, and Swampert.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

Mitonga Island's Odd Stones

This Quest can be received from Casey near the western exit to Tilt Village after completing Mission 14: Protect the Peace of Oblivia!

To complete this Quest, the player must speak to Casey to go to Noir Forest and use Entei to clear all the rocks there, and then capture Golem.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

The Explorer's Report

This Quest can be received from the Ruin Maniac in the Canal Ruins, near the exit to Sophian Road, after starting Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture an Azumarill at the Silver Falls and bring it to the Ruin Maniac.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

The Explorer's Letter

This Quest can be received from the Ruin Maniac in the Canal Ruins, near the exit to Sophian Road, after starting Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture a Floatzel in the Oblivia Ruins and bring it to the Ruin Maniac.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

The Precious Red Berry

This Quest can be received from Booker in his house in Cocona Village after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must get a red berry from the tree in the northeastern part of Mitonga Road, outside the northern exit to the Daybreak Ruins, and then return it to Booker. Tackle x1 must be used to clear the Forest Tree to get the berry.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

Ralph's Bright Idea

This Quest can be received from Nick in Booker's house in Cocona Village after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must:

  1. Go to Rand's House to see Nema
  2. Go to Nick's bridge on Latolato Trail to see Ralph
  3. Go to the storehouse on Hinder Cape, near the Big Booker Bridge, to try to find Ralph
  4. Go to Nick's bridge on Latolato Trail to see Ralph and Nema

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points.

Dolce Island's Pichu

This Quest can be received from Booker in his house in Cocona Village after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must locate and capture six of the Pichu from Dolce Island, and bring them back to Booker's house. They are located:

  1. Near the sign next to the save machine outside Booker's house; Raikou, Entei, or Suicune's roar must be used to unveil it
  2. In the Forest Tree in Teakwood Forest, near the exit to Rasp Cavern; Tackle x1 must be used to clear the target
  3. The player must go to Rand's house, then locate a Pichu on Mt. Latolato, next to the bridge leading to the Wireless Tower
  4. At the eastern end of the Dangerous Cliff
  5. The player must go to the Aqua Resort's central square, then locate a Pichu at the Silver Falls, near the entrance to the Oblivia Ruins
  6. At Faldera Volcano

The reward for completing this quest is 30 Ranger Points.

The Forbidden Song

This Quest can be received from Rand in his house after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must go to the Old Mansion and the search the upper-right shelf in the library for a book, The Forbidden Song, and then return to Rand.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points. After completing the quest, going to Tilikule Island will also allow the player to capture Latias/Latios.

Power for Nema

This Quest can be received from Nema in Rand's House after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture Electivire, Jolteon, Manectric, Ampharos, and Electrode and bring them to Nema. Jolteon can be found at the Wireless Tower, and the other Pokémon can be found on Mt. Layuda.

The reward for completing this quest is 25 Ranger Points. After completing the quest, the Many Times, Anytime Capture Machine will become available.

Ranger Contest!

This Quest can be received from Murph at the port in Tilt Village after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must capture Togekiss, Honchkrow, Probopass, Kabutops, Quagsire, Heracross, and Cherrim and bring them to Murph.

  • Togekiss and Honchkrow must be captured in the Sky, and then the player must speak to Supurna to add them to their land party.
  • Probopass is on Mitonga Road, outside the exit to the Daybreak Ruins.
  • Kabutops is on the Dangerous Cliff.
  • Quagsire is on the island in the pond east of the Old Mansion. Suicune is required to access it.
  • Heracross is in the Canal Ruins.
  • Cherrim is in the flower garden on Latolato Trail. Suicune is required to access it, and Raikou, Entei, or Suicune's roar must be used to unveil it.

The reward for completing this quest is 30 Ranger Points.

The Sad Suicune

This Quest can be received from Weber in the northwestern house in the Aqua Resort after defeating Red Eyes after completing Mission 15: Calm the Sky's Rage!

To complete this Quest, the player must:

  1. Talk to Leanne in Rand's House
  2. Go to the Monument at the exit to the Daybreak Ruins and restore the Involith
  3. Go to the Monument in the lake in the Canal Ruins and restore the Involith
  4. Receive the Involith from Ben at the summit of Mt. Layuda
  5. Go to the Monument in Rasp Cavern and restore the Involith
  6. Return to Weber

    7. The reward for completing this quest is 30 Ranger Points.

    Go-Rock Oblivia!

    This Quest can be received from the Manager at the port in the Aqua Resort after completing all other Quests.

    To complete this Quest, the player must:

    1. Find Garrett on the first avalanche-prone slope of Mt. Sorbet
    2. Find Clyde in the middle of the Big Booker Bridge
    3. Find Clyde on Hinder Cape and speak with him to go to Latolato Trail
    4. Capture a Sneasel and two Weavile on Latolato Trail
    5. Go to the Oblivia Ruins to find Billy
    6. Capture a Lucario in the Oblivia Ruins

    The reward for completing this quest is 50 Ranger Points.
