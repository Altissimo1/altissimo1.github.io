Bank Shutdown

On 13 Aug 2026, it was announced that Pokémon Bank, the 3DS application that allows players to move Pokémon around gens 5-7 and from the 3DS to the Switch, will be shut down on 26 February 2027. This was inevitable, but the fact that a date has been announced means that many players may be scrambling to complete goals within their Pokémon games prior to this date.

To that end, I am working on two pages to address the situation:

  • A list of Pokémon (forms, shiny variants, moves, abilities, ball combinations) that will become unavailable in any Switch (2) game, or Pokémon HOME, after the shutdown, unless transferred via Bank.
  • An explanation of what the shutdown of Bank means for transferring Pokémon around DS and 3DS games and completing Pokédexes in those games, even without taking into account the loss of transfer ability to the Switch.

I will be working on those as I have time today. The first page I am sourcing contributions from via a post I made on Smogon and I will be supplementing this with my own knowledge as I do some research into the subject.

As always, feel free to contact me if you want to add to this discussion or if there's anything you want me to answer specifically regarding this.

The same announcement also came with a confirmation that the Switch releases of FireRed and LeafGreen will be gaining HOME support in October, accompanied by a gift Celebi in Pokémon HOME for completing the Pokédex (I am presently unsure if this refers to the Kanto Pokédex or the National Pokédex). I will write more about that when the support is properly added.

Champions Resource

Pokémon Champions was released on 8 April for the Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2. Because this game has little in the way of single-player content, and because a lot of the data that I would want to cover (such as information about rental Pokémon generation) is handled on the server and thus cannot be datamined, I probably won't cover this game unless some future update adds single-player content.

That having been said, I am still open to pull requests or emails to add pages about Champions that would be useful for players. To that end, I have added a Champions page; currently, it only links to off-site resources about the currently-available Pokémon and about the generation of recruitable Pokémon. In the future, Daddy Buzzwole (the creator of the recruitable Pokémon documentation) and I may collaborate on presenting his information using my site.

Games

  • Red, Green, Blue, and Yellow

Supplementary Series: A term I made up to describe games that don't have the exact same gameplay loop as core games, but can trade and interact with them. Currently contains pages for the following games:

  • Legends: Arceus
  • Legends: Z-A (currently a work in progress)
  • Stadium series, including Battle Revolution
  • Orre games (Colosseum and XD)
  • Champions

Side Series: Other Pokémon games with altogether different mechanics. Currently contains pages for the following games:

  • Pinball series
  • Mystery Dungeon series
  • Ranger series
  • Trozei / Link series
